# Courtside: European Basketball Manager

A browser basketball management game in development. The design targets the top two men's divisions in Turkey, Spain, Greece, Germany, Italy and France, plus EuroLeague, EuroCup and Basketball Champions League.

The playable development version includes a **fictional six-club league with season rollover and offseason recruitment** and standalone exhibitions. Choose a club, coach its home and away games, complete ten matchdays, renew contracts and recruit for the following year. Review current and retained past-season player statistics, box scores, tables and results. The possession engine includes passing, drives, defensive matchups, a tested subset of FIBA 2024 rules, a large animated diagonal court and live stamina bars. The game opens at a main menu with New career, Continue, Load saves, Import and Exhibition. New careers collect your manager profile and club before opening the manager's desk. The full historical management career remains in development.

Real reference data is now available separately: **double-click `Open real data.cmd`** in this folder to browse 40 clubs and 727 players from official 2025-26 EuroLeague/EuroCup records. No account or payment is needed. Search, player statistics and spreadsheet exports work offline after downloading. See [coverage and source details](docs/real-data.md); these records are not yet integrated into the career game.

## Run locally

Requires Node.js 24.14 or newer.

On Windows, double-click **Start game.cmd** to launch the game in your browser. Keep its window open while playing; closing it stops the local server. It installs the locked dependencies if they are missing.

```sh
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the local URL printed by Vite. Choose **New career**, enter your manager name, date of birth and nationality, select a club, then review the injury setting and press **Start career**. Nationality offers suggestions while accepting your own text, including multiple nationalities. A fresh replay code is chosen automatically; **Advanced setup → Replay code** optionally accepts a code to reproduce the same random events with the same starting options and decisions. It is not a difficulty setting. The available pack is the fictional development league beginning 5 September 2026.

After each finished match, **Continue** records the matchday results and prepares the next fixture. After finalizing the last matchday, **Review offseason** opens a dated recruitment window. Send offers and **Continue offseason** to the next response or expiry; every club needs eight signed players and no outstanding offers before the following year can start. Careers with contracts disabled retain **Next season**. The Season selector in Competitions opens earlier tables and results. **Autosave** records a checkpoint when a new game starts, after each completed matchday, after season rollover, and after contract/scouting decisions and recruitment-calendar advances. **Save season** also saves during a match. Open **Save history** in the footer to restore one of the five most recent checkpoints; **Continue saved career** on the main menu opens the latest.

**Main menu** pauses and saves an active career; **Resume current career** returns to it. Click your manager identity in the sidebar, or **Manager profile** in the footer, to edit your details. Date of birth determines age on the simulated calendar. Older saves can add a profile without restarting. Export/import still works for file backups; local history retains five snapshots across careers, so export a separate file to keep a career permanently.

Choose Standard, Reduced or Off injuries when starting a game; the setting applies to every club. Injuries carry between fixtures, recovering players are kept out of the lineup, and Squad shows a medical report with return dates and history. Original format-1 season saves retain injuries off when loaded.

The shared budget strip appears above every career workspace: **Remaining budget**, **Remaining staff budget**, and **Player salary budget**. These are existing cash and annual commitment limits, not money to add together. Open **Budget details** for player offers on hold, signed wages, staff commitments and the minimum-squad reserve. Pending player offers reserve annual salary room and a squad place in each covered year. Paid club transfers also reserve the offered fee from the remaining budget; cash moves only when the player accepts. Staff and salary limits remain separate.

To substitute, drag an eligible reserve onto an active player, or select the reserve and then the player coming off using mouse, touch or keyboard. The game pauses when you begin; changes apply immediately only when legal, otherwise they appear in the cancellable queue. The large court stays below the score, with the lineup, stamina and coaching controls beneath it.

**Training** opens weekly plans for your club. Delegate to the assistant or choose Recovery, Light, Standard or Heavy workload, a team focus, and individual player focuses. The week runs after you finish a matchday and press Continue; heavier work trades freshness for development. Injured players miss unavailable days. The latest report shows participation, gains and condition changes. Older careers have an **Enable training** button. Use **Save season** to save plan edits before the next automatic checkpoint.

**Staff → Fitness coach** recruits a coach to improve weekly condition recovery and reduce fatigue from practice. Candidate reviews show their actual effects and season fee. Training shows the appointed coach and the completed week's measured condition benefit for each player; fully rested players can gain zero. Effects apply only on medically available days during the nine training weeks. Injury return dates and offseason recovery stay unchanged. Release keeps earlier reports and provides no refund. New careers enable the role; older careers can enable it in Staff. No new appointment is offered after the last training week.

**Squad → Season statistics** shows sortable per-game, total and per-40-minute figures. Select a player to review their match history, including injury absences and unused games. **Competitions → League player statistics** compares all clubs with position, club, name and participation filters; player reports link to match box scores. These figures use committed results, so they update after Continue. Use the season selector in Squad or Competitions to revisit archived player statistics and match box scores. Newly completed seasons retain player records. Archives imported from formats 1-8 have team results only; their missing player statistics are shown as unavailable.

**Staff** has Training coaches, Scouts and Fitness coach views, with three training-coach positions, three scouts and one fitness coach, illustrated portraits, stars, skill bars and direct renewal/release controls. Browse available candidates, then review the full season fee and remaining staff budget before hiring. All three roles share the existing EUR 150,000 season allocation and spend actual cash; released fees remain committed with no refund. Coaches improve future practice. Each scout adds one observation per matchday and improves the evidence in their reports. Scout and fitness-coach decisions save automatically; Save season keeps training-coach edits before the next checkpoint. Older careers can enable the corresponding role explicitly; scout recruitment requires finances and scouting, and fitness coaching requires finances and training.

**Club** manages ticket prices and the club ledger. Set a price or delegate ticketing, inspect the next home gate forecast, and review income, costs, cash and outstanding obligations. The current matchday's price is locked; edits apply when the next matchday is prepared. Continue books home ticket income and operating costs once. Training-coach, scout and fitness-coach fees are paid from cash as well as consuming the shared staff allocation; unpaid costs block new appointments. Older careers can enable finances explicitly. Use Save season to keep ticket edits before the next checkpoint.

**Club / Cash outlook** shows upcoming wages, operating costs, agreed transfer payments and estimated ticket income through this season. Select a point or switch between Expected and Cautious to inspect the next payment and lowest balance. Cautious assumes 25% less ticket income and delayed sale income. A funding gap is a planning warning; Remaining budget still controls what you can spend. Pending offers and later-season installments are shown separately. Posted/prepaid wages and earlier transfer receipts are not charged twice. Detailed rows and assumptions stay collapsed until needed; the manager's desk links to an expected cash shortfall.

**Squad → Wages** shows each player's season wage, charges and remaining commitment. Wages are charged in ten matchday installments, including for injured or unused players. Settle a player's remaining season wages early for a 5% discount, or delegate settlement decisions. Automatic payroll preserves the other players' remaining wages and a EUR 100,000 cash buffer. Staff recruitment also respects future wage commitments. Older careers can enable payroll after enabling club finances; charges begin prospectively. Save season keeps payroll decisions before the next automatic checkpoint.

**Transfers** manages employment contracts and next season's squad. Renew expiring players during the season; after the final matchday, recruit available players from the development league. Review annual wages and one-to-three-year terms before signing. Every club has a EUR 240,000 annual wage allocation and must register eight to twelve players. Budget checks protect room to complete an eight-player squad. Initial contracts expire after the current season; accepted renewals and signings take effect next season, leaving current matches and registrations unchanged. During the active season, **Assistant** renews players toward a squad of ten. In the dated offseason it sends ordinary pending offers and waits for the same scheduled player replies as you. Signed contracts remain binding if you return to Manual; outstanding offers remain until resolved, replaced, withdrawn or expired. Contract activation, delegation changes and accepted offers save automatically. New careers enable contracts; imported formats 1–9 retain an **Enable player contracts** action after finances and payroll are enabled.

**Scouting** opens on your shortlist, with **Discover** and **Reports** views. Filter by name, position, club and minimum/maximum age; ages come from stable fictional birth dates and the simulated date, with missing biographies left unknown. Add a player to the shortlist, choose **Observe**, then review the report after Continue commits the matchday; the report links to the player's contract view. Shortlisting alone does not commission a report. Two club observations remain available without hired scouts. Each hired scout adds one named observation per matchday, up to five total. Choose Best available, a named scout or Club observation. Releasing a scout cancels their pending observation and retains their completed reports. A player needs court time to improve your knowledge; unused players produce a **No minutes** review. Successful observations improve report depth through three levels, while outsider skills always remain ranges. Reports keep their assessment date and widen as they age. Your own squad shows exact current skills; departing players retain an uncertain assessment from their time at your club. Switch assignments to **Assistant** to fill available slots automatically. Assignment, cancellation, shortlist and delegation decisions save automatically. New careers enable scouting; imported formats 1–10 keep it disabled until you choose **Enable scouting**. New observations require an active season and a registered player, so they cannot be assigned during the offseason or to unattached free agents.

Hired scouts use judging and reporting skills to improve report depth sooner; no minutes still means no knowledge gain. Reports retain their observer and quality at assignment, so later hiring or release cannot rewrite an earlier assessment. AI recruitment uses its own exact squad knowledge, saved outsider estimates or a neutral estimate for unknown players. Regional searches, potential/personality assessments and calibrated observation accuracy remain future work; no unmodeled personal traits are invented.

**Contract negotiations** in Transfers now return acceptance, a counteroffer or a rejection, with a saved conversation history. The provisional rule asks the base annual wage for one year, 5% more for two years and 10% more for three years, rounded up to EUR 500. For example, an EUR 18,000 base requirement becomes EUR 19,000 for two years or EUR 20,000 for three. Offers below 75% of the base requirement are rejected; other insufficient offers receive a wage counter and, where possible, a shorter term at your proposed wage. Loading a counter into the controls does not sign it. **Submit offer** can immediately sign an accepted active-season renewal; during the dated offseason it sends a reserved proposal whose acceptance signs on the scheduled response day; rejected and countered offers create no wage commitment or cash charge. Each response saves automatically, and identical immediate retries do not create another response. New careers enable negotiations; imported formats 1–11 retain their old contract behavior until **Enable contract negotiations** is selected after employment is enabled. Existing contracts stay binding. AI and assistant recruitment use the same rules and journal.

**Offseason calendar** opens after the final matchday for 28 simulated days in this fictional development league. A proposal receives a reply one day after submission. Pending proposals and counteroffers hold the originally offered annual wage and one squad place until withdrawn, replaced or expired, at most seven days after submission and never beyond the window. A counteroffer does not silently raise the reserved amount; reviewing revised terms rechecks affordability. Continue stops at the next response or expiry, otherwise up to seven days later. Accepted replies sign automatically under the submitted terms; no cash signing fee is charged.

Several clubs can now bid for the same available player. On the reply date, the player chooses an acceptable offer by annual salary, then contract length, then staying at his current club; a repeatable tie-break decides otherwise equal offers. The chosen contract signs automatically. Other live bids become **Chose another club** and release their salary budget and squad place. AI clubs and your assistant use the same offer, reservation and response commands. You may start next season early once every club has at least eight signed players and all proposals are resolved or withdrawn. Advancing to closure is blocked while a squad is incomplete, leaving the prior decision point available to improve terms or delegate recruitment. Calendar advances recover condition without rewriting the completed match. New careers enable the calendar; formats 1-12 keep their previous immediate offseason behavior until **Enable dated offseason** in Transfers. Saves now use format 22. Older careers preserve their existing reports and pending observations, with recruitable scouts and fitness coaches disabled unless those systems were already present or are enabled in Staff. Format-13 offers retain their original rules until all existing live proposals resolve; competing bids then activate automatically. Agents, personal preferences and richer contract clauses remain future work.

**Club transfers** adds contracted targets to Transfers during the competitive offseason. Submit a transfer fee together with the proposed salary and contract length. The selling club responds first; only an approved fee moves to the player's dated decision. Incoming offers for your players require Accept, Reject or a fee counter, unless contract management is delegated. A club's provisional asking fee is its remaining contracted salary, rounded up to EUR 1,000, with offers between EUR 1,000 and EUR 500,000. Approval alone spends no cash. When the player accepts, the buyer pays the first installment, the seller receives that actual payment, and the new employment begins next season. Earlier terms and match records remain intact. Withdrawal, expiry, rejection or losing to a rival release fee and salary holds. Clubs protect replacement capacity and deadline time; AI can seek a stronger known contracted replacement after selling, using the same approval, cash and salary rules. Choose **Pay in full**, **2 payments** (half now, half next opening day), or **3 payments** (half now, a quarter at opening and a quarter at matchday six). The review shows real dates and amounts. The full unpaid fee remains reserved in Remaining budget, while the payment schedule shows upcoming and overdue balances. Due payments use cash after protected player wages; shortfalls pay partially and settle from later real income. Seller income appears only when paid. Paid in-season moves are described below; loans and broader contract mechanics remain future work. Formats 1-15 load without changing previous cash or results; existing paid transfers become fully settled upfront schedules.

**In-season transfers** let free agents join for the remaining season and contracted players join after their club agrees a fee. Approved offers receive the player's reply before the next matchday. Paid contracts can cover this season plus up to two more, using any of the three payment plans; later installments fall in the following season. Review and decide incoming club offers before continuing, or delegate recruitment. Signing changes both squads and starts the buyer's wages for the new matchday. The seller's already paid wages remain a non-refundable historical cost. The current match and previous records stay intact. Older saves retain paid in-season transfers disabled until you enable them in Transfers.

**Release clauses** are optional when agreeing a new contract or renewal. The review shows the release amount and its effect on the requested salary: a three-season deal has a 5% premium with a clause, instead of 10% without one. Existing contracts are unchanged. When a target has an applicable clause, choose **Release clause** to offer its exact amount upfront and go directly to the player's decision. The player can still refuse or choose another club; budget and squad rules still apply. A separate future agreement cannot be erased using the current agreement's clause. Sellers see the approach without fee-approval buttons. Ordinary fee negotiations and installment plans remain available. This is a provisional development-league policy; formats 1-19 load without adding clauses or changing old offers.

**Contract buyouts** are available in Transfers for your next-season contracted players during the competitive offseason. Review the full remaining salary settlement, salary budget freed, next squad size and Remaining budget after payment before confirming. Compensation is paid once; the released player becomes available to other clubs for next season. Your club cannot re-sign him in the same recruitment window, and an assistant will not undo the release. Current results, registrations and salary payments remain intact. Any outstanding transfer installments still have to be paid. The release checks cash, pending commitments, overdue costs and room to complete an eight-player squad. This development policy pays all remaining guaranteed salary; it is not a jurisdiction-specific negotiated buyout clause. Formats 1-16 load without inventing releases, and format-16 installment receipts remain unchanged.

Training, Club, Transfers and the manager's desk keep the current decision visible, with explanations, detailed accounts, previous reports and conversation history behind disclosures. Displayed player ages do not yet affect development, performance or retirement. This usability update adds no real roster data, new economy or match rules. Validation is recorded in [implementation status](docs/implementation-status.md#verification-completed).

Build static assets with `npm run build`; the output is `dist/`. Deployment remains under the project owner's control.

In Windows PowerShell, use `npm.cmd` in place of `npm` if execution policy blocks `npm.ps1`.

```sh
npm test
npm run typecheck
npm run build
npm run benchmark:match
```

## Project boundaries

The exhibition supports watching/pausing, quarter skipping, instant simulation, tactical changes, queued substitutions and timeouts, foul-outs, team penalties, shot-clock resets, basic injuries, assistant rotations, squad profiles, box scores, and local save/import/export. Mandatory replacements pause every playback mode until resolved. New exhibitions resolve separate advances, completed passes, drives and shots. Court spacing, the assigned defender, player attributes and fatigue influence shot creation. Completed passes can lead to makes or misses; qualifying made baskets credit the actual passer with an assist. The court animates those recorded actions alongside free throws, rebounds, turnovers and substitutions; reduced-motion and text-only views use the same events. Coaching feedback reports completed passes, drives and shot contests. These are discrete action sequences; full movement physics, competition-specific rules and statistical calibration remain later work. See the status document for precise limits and verification results.

Exhibition saves use format 2. New matches use `possession-3` and save the live handler, passing context, positions and defensive assignments. Earlier `rules-2` matches retain their original engine and deterministic continuation; starting a new exhibition selects the new engine. Court scenes remain optional presentation metadata, with a static fallback for events without scenes. Saves restore the resolved sporting state; a partly watched animation resumes at that action's completed state. Format-1 prototype saves remain unsupported and are not overwritten on load. This development save format is not yet a stable career format.

Season files use a separate format-20 `basketball-manager-season` envelope. Local season storage retains five transactional checkpoints, separate from exhibitions. They retain the manager profile, active match, club instructions, lineup, condition, career injury history, current schedule, completed box scores, training, coaching contracts, the financial ledger, wage agreements, full employment history, scouting observations, shortlists, negotiation conversations and dated recruitment command/offer histories. New archives retain player box scores, registered roster snapshots, jersey numbers, scores and final standings. Previously discarded box scores in old saves cannot be recovered. Valid formats 1 to 12 migrate without changing their active match or random state, with the offseason calendar disabled until explicitly enabled. Formats 1 to 11 also retain disabled negotiations; format 12 preserves its response journal. Formats 1–10 keep scouting disabled until enabled; format 11 retains it. Formats 1–9 also keep player contracts disabled until enabled; later formats retain their employment state. Formats 1 to 7 keep the manager profile unset until supplied; later versions retain it. Original roster ownership is reconstructed for old results and reports, while archived player statistics discarded by formats 1–8 remain unavailable. Formats 1–3 keep training disabled until enabled; formats 1–4 keep coaching disabled until enabled; formats 1–5 keep finances disabled until enabled; formats 1–6 keep player payroll disabled until enabled. Existing management state is preserved. Formats 1-17 preserve their recorded history and receive disabled in-season recruitment with round-one metadata on existing employment agreements. Formats 1-18 retain paid in-season transfers disabled until explicitly enabled; format-18 free-agent arrivals keep their actual registration rounds, wages and journals. Season saves do not overwrite the exhibition slot. Matchdays and season transitions run in a cancellable worker and commit once. Each new year begins on the first Saturday of September, applying offseason recovery and contracted roster changes together. All sixty catalog players retain their identities, condition and development even while unattached. Aging, the full club economy and detailed rehabilitation remain in development. Injury incidence and recovery durations remain provisional game models.

If another tab saves newer progress, this tab cannot overwrite it: load the latest checkpoint to continue, or export the current game to preserve a different timeline. Failed automatic saves leave the current game open and show a persistent recovery message. The five checkpoints share one local season slot across clubs; use file exports to retain separate careers. Autosave happens at game creation, calendar boundaries and contract/scouting decisions, not after every match action.

The initial training model is provisional: 100 practice points raise a skill by one, with a maximum gain of two per skill and a rating ceiling of 20. Assigned coaches can add bounded practice gains; facilities, age, latent potential and training injuries do not affect it yet. Training runs between matchdays 1–10; offseason rollover grants recovery but no skill points. Assistant, fitness, physio, scout and youth-coach recruitment remain future systems.

The financial model uses fictional venues, sponsors and estimates: EUR 500,000 opening cash, EUR 200,000 annual sponsorship, EUR 15,000 operating costs per matchday, full-season coaching fees and authored starting wages. Midseason finance activation prorates sponsorship and charges existing current-year coaching contracts. Receipts settle unpaid costs before adding available cash; there is no free overdraft. Attendance depends on price, capacity and earlier results using provisional coefficients. Payroll uses a ten-installment development schedule, not the planned monthly payroll calendar; each new schedule uses that year's agreed employment wage. Wage charges can create unpaid club obligations and are not necessarily cash payments. The negotiation model uses authored one-year wage requirements and modest premiums for longer commitments, with offers capped at EUR 50,000 in EUR 500 steps and no upfront signing fee. Transfers support offseason free-agent signings, nonrenewal departures, consent-based paid moves with installments and compensated buyouts of future contracts. In-season free agents can sign for the rest of the season after a next-matchday reply; cash, annual salary capacity and squad places are reserved, and only remaining wage installments are charged. Paid in-season moves now include club consent, contracts covering the rest of this season plus up to two more, and all three payment plans. Seller wages stop prospectively; already prepaid wages remain a non-refundable historical cost. Negotiated release clauses, loans, agents, regional scouting networks, sponsorship negotiations, facilities, debt recovery and board sanctions remain unimplemented. Finances do not constitute a calibrated full-club economy yet.

The agreed first release includes all twelve domestic divisions and the three European competitions. Smaller development fixtures do not reduce that scope. Realistic statistical behavior, the historical data pack and full competition rules require validation before release.

The code's Apache license does not grant rights to third-party player photos, club marks or datasets. The development fixture uses fictional identities and code-drawn appearance. Real data and images need separate source/asset manifests; estimates must be labeled.
