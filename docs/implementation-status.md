## Level 7 acceptance (13 November 2026)

- Overpricing: above the sell-out price, attendance falls faster than the price rises (elasticity 1.4), so overcharging loses crowd and income; the automatic price maximizes gate revenue (`tests/level7-acceptance.test.ts`, `tests/finance.test.ts`).
- No free money: sponsor payments never exceed their agreements (or the fixed EUR 200,000 before negotiations), board capital is approved immediately before the upgrade it funds, stays within the season's owner capital and at most the upgrade cost (plus the documented EUR 150,000 cushion for automatic requests), and each project is paid exactly once; the strict save check replays all of it.
- Automatic control: every construction start is covered by the club's cash; automatic sponsor signings and board capital decisions appear in the Inbox, and every payment appears in the Club ledger.
- Dismissal: the world keeps all clubs, squads, results and history; the former club continues under automatic control with a full squad and the career continues at the new club.
- Health run (three all-automatic five-season careers, seeds 3, 7 and 227): 8 of 90 club-seasons ended Under pressure, 2 dismissals in 15 manager-seasons with both careers continuing, arenas 93-94% full, sponsorship EUR 171,000-192,000 per club-season, no club with negative cash, one club with EUR 11,775 unpaid, 6.2 MB saves validating in 0.75-1.2 s.

## Implemented: dismissal and job changes

- Groundwork (format 37): a career can record a change of club at the end of a season, after the final board review; the new club runs that offseason and later seasons, and the former club continues under automatic control. The save checker credits every earlier decision (sponsorship, facilities, academy, press, squad conversations, recruitment) to the club managed at that time.
- Dismissal and offers (format 38): with the setting on (new careers), a season that ends Under pressure dismisses the manager. Clubs whose season also went badly replace their coach and offer the job according to the manager's reputation (the last three final board ratings); a dismissed manager always receives an offer.
- Continuing after the final match shows the verdict and the offers before the matchday is recorded: join a club, stay (when not dismissed) or decide later. Club > Board > Your job shows reputation, whether the job is safe or at risk, and every club managed; older careers switch it on there, from the next season end. The manager's desk warns when the job is at risk, and the Inbox records club changes and declined offers. Unemployed seasons, mid-season dismissals and a protected first season remain suggestions.

## Implemented: facility condition and upkeep plans

- Club > Operations > Facilities shows a condition bar for each upgraded facility and an **Upkeep** switch (Full or Reduced) with its effect and when it starts. The manager's desk and the Inbox flag facilities below 60 condition. Older careers use **Enable upkeep choices**.
- Reduced upkeep halves the cost while upgraded facilities lose 5 condition per matchday; full upkeep recovers 10 per matchday; a completed upgrade restores 100. Condition scales the practice bonus and the extra arena seats. Automatic control and rival clubs reduce upkeep only when committed wages and fees exceed cash or costs are unpaid.
- Format 36 records the opt-in anchor and each club's dated upkeep choices; gates, training reports and upkeep payments are rebuilt from them. Financing, leases, medical/scouting facilities and repair projects remain future work.

## Explicit limits of this milestone

This is a FIBA 2024 exhibition subset, not certification for all planned competitions. EuroLeague has verified differences in jump balls, period possession and technical-foul totals. The [rules evidence matrix](design/rules-coverage.md) separates tested scenarios from partial or missing coverage.

Technical/unsportsmanlike/disqualifying fouls, combined penalties, reviews, lane violations, goaltending, separately timed release/flight adjudication, three-/five-/eight-second violations, all throw-in locations and the late-game frontcourt advance choice remain unimplemented. Possession creation and shot resolution remain discrete actions. Authoritative spacing and assigned defenders now affect outcomes, but continuous movement, collisions, screens, help rotations, switches and zone defense remain unimplemented. The current three-point classification uses basket distance and authored perimeter positions that avoid the corner-line exception; full arbitrary-location court adjudication remains open. The deterministic injury and fatigue models, tactical coefficients and player strength require statistical calibration.

The fictional development league supports consecutive seasons, employment expiry, salary/term offers and offseason free-agent recruitment. There is no configured real domestic/European league, complete career model, game-ready historical database, full commercial/facilities system, complete training/development model, regional scouting networks or complete board/press systems yet. Youth fixtures and physio-supported rehabilitation are implemented as provisional foundations. The original sixty seniors now share a career-local population with academy graduates and annual youth intake. Age-adjusted practice, gradual calendar decline and retirement now operate alongside calendar ages; long-run population balance and the richer development model still need calibration. Unimplemented management systems are omitted from navigation. Acquired reference records have not been converted into career squads or skill ratings.
