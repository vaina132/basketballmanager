# Club and competition logos

- Source: API-Sports (team and league logos, media.api-sports.io), fetched on 22 November 2026 for the 2026-27 season.
- Made by `scripts/real-data-logos.mjs`: each real-pack club matched to its API-Sports team, resized to 96x96 WebP. The map is `src/data/real-logos.ts`.
- The logos are trademarks of their clubs and competitions and belong to them. They are shown only to identify the clubs and competitions in the game.
- A logo is removed on request.
- 23 November 2026: Hestia Menorca added from its earlier API-Sports team (Vive Menorca, the same club, Menorca Bàsquet), merged by hand so no existing logo was dropped. The other 28 clubs without a logo and the Türkiye Basketbol Ligi have none at API-Sports (missing image or the shared placeholder; the TBL entry shows the Süper Ligi mark).
- Clubs without a logo here (none at API-Sports, or only a placeholder) keep the game's plain badge.
