# NBA technical plan (23 November 2026)

Facts the plan relies on: a pack competition has only `country`, `tier`, `kind`; `careerCompetitions()` (`src/world/real-world.ts`) offers leagues of 4-20 clubs and `validateWorld` (`src/world/career-world.ts`) refuses more than 20, so a 30-club NBA in the pack is invisible to deployed clients until code unlocks it. New rules are optional world markers set by `ruledWorld()` and guarded per format in `src/storage/season-storage.ts`. Domestic leagues are double round robins, one round per Saturday; `buildCompetitionCalendar`/`seriesGames` refuse games less than 2 days apart. Best-of-seven, 2-2-1-1-1 and the 7v8/9v10 play-in exist in `src/world/postseason.ts`. The engine hard-codes FIBA (600 s quarters and 2400 in `src/engine/match.ts`; foul-out 5; bonus at 5; timeouts 2/3/1; `plays.ts` bonus/foul-out/three-point spot; `possession.ts` three at 6.75; validators `validate-match.ts`, `season-storage.ts`, `senior-history-validation.ts`; `performance.ts` per-40; `viewer/flow.ts` clock and bonus). Money is whole euro cents; `formatEuros` (`src/ui/money-format.ts`) in about 38 UI files; `REAL_ECONOMY_RULES` in `src/world/economy.ts`; `MAX_CONTRACT_SEASONS = 3`. No trades, draft or external free-agent pool. Career: `vacancyRequirement`, `clubLevel()` = tier (`src/world/career-policy.ts`). Ratings: `scripts/real-data-ratings.mjs` (`rating-3`), position balance in `supabase/functions/publish-starter-pack/rating-balance.ts`.

All NBA rules sit behind the world marker `world.nba`, set only by a new option `nba` in `NEW_CAREER_SYSTEMS`. Until step 5 is done a constant `NBA_PLAYABLE = false` hides the league in the career setup.

1. **Data.** API-Sports league 12 (raw downloads in the session scratchpad `nba/raw`). Conferences/divisions (30 rows), arenas and capacities, positions/heights/birth dates (Wikipedia rosters, as for Europe), head coaches (`src/data/real-coaches.ts`), logos (`scripts/real-data-logos.mjs`), photos (`scripts/real-data-player-photos.mjs`), published team payrolls (USD) for `src/world/nba-economy.ts`.
2. **Ratings and pack.** `rating-4`: Europe solved exactly as `rating-3`, then the NBA level fitted against fixed European levels (players in both, 2025-26 NBA -> 2026-27 Europe movers at half weight); own prior and wider ranges for the NBA only. `publish-starter-pack` v8: `competition.groups` (conferences, divisions with `parentId`), `club.capacity`; position balance run separately for USA. Check: every non-NBA player identical to world-13.
3. **Engine rulesets.** First `tests/fixtures/fiba-golden.json` (hashes of about 60 seeded matches across rules/possession/plays models and youth) and `tests/match-ruleset-golden.test.ts`. Then `src/engine/rulesets.ts` (`fiba-2024`, `nba-2026`, `rulesOf(state)`: absent = FIBA), replace the constants, NBA-only random draws only on the NBA path, `calibrationFor(ruleset)`, `FixtureMatchOptions.ruleset` derived from the fixture's league, validators read `rulesOf`, viewer clock/bonus/court. Guard: `ruleset` refused before its format and when it does not match the league. Tests: `nba-match-rules`, `nba-match-calibration`.
4. **World builder.** `DataPackCompetition.groups?`, `DataPackClub.capacity?`; `careerCompetitions` allows USA with 30 clubs; `CareerWorld.nba = {model, leagueId, conferences, ruleset}`; `validateWorld` accepts it; USA has no lower division, no national team windows (players miss FIBA windows); NBA clubs have no academy or youth teams (`clubHasAcademy`); facilities start high; presidents shown as "Owner".
5. **Schedule and postseason.** `src/world/nba-schedule.ts` (82 games: 4x4 division, 6x4 + 4x3 conference with the 3-game opponents rotating by season, 15x2 other conference; 41 home; seeded greedy day fill, back-to-backs allowed, at most 16, never 3 in 3 days; cached in `pure-cache.ts`). Minimum-rest parameter in `competition-calendar.ts`; `domesticSchedule` uses it; `expectedRegularGames(world, league)` replaces n(n-1) assumptions; `regularWorldRounds` excludes the NBA. `src/world/nba-ranking.ts` (rule `nba-2026`, two-team and multi-team tie-breaks, tables per conference/division/league). Postseason format `nba-2026-1`: per-conference play-in and bracket, conference finals, Finals by league record; slot every second day. Then `NBA_PLAYABLE = true`.
6. **Economy and currency.** Display setting euros/dollars (`formatMoney`, a currency context; stored money stays euro cents). Band `nba` in `economy.ts` (revenue about $300-750M, payroll near each team's published total; the NBA pays far more than Europe). `src/world/nba-cap.ts` and `nba-cap-policy.ts`: cap, mid-level exception, simplified Bird rights, minimum/maximum by age band, rookie scale, roster 13-15, luxury tax at the end of the regular season shared among non-payers; owner's payroll ceiling from president traits.
7. **Free agency and NBA-Europe moves.** NBA clubs bid under the cap policy; players with NBA value above a threshold prefer NBA offers; Europe-to-NBA buyouts up to the CBA allowance; unsigned players go to Europe in combined worlds.
8. **Draft.** `src/world/nba-draft.ts`: about 90 generated prospects a year, lottery (2019 odds, 4 drawn picks), 2 x 30 picks, manager picks or delegates, rookie scale; `state.draft` journal; re-derived lottery and computer picks.
9. **Trades.** `src/world/trades.ts`: player for player, salary matching, rosters 13-15, deadline first Thursday of February, Inbox answers, about 4-8 computer trades a season at fixed dates; `state.trades` journal.
10. **Career.** Prestige order NBA > EuroLeague > tier 1 > tier 2; NBA vacancies need reputation 60-70; displaced real NBA coaches become free; owners judge wins and the playoffs.
11. **Screens.** NBA tab in Competitions (League tab when managing there), conference/division/league standings with seed marks, bracket (`NbaBracket.tsx`), draft (`NbaDraft.tsx`), trades (`TradeBuilder.tsx`), cap sheet (`CapSheet.tsx`), "Game 23/82", "Q4 12:00", NBA court.
12. **Measurement.** NBA calibration targets per team per game: points 114, pace 99, FG 47%, 37 three-point attempts at 36%, FTA 21.5 at 78%, rebounds 44, assists 26.5, turnovers 14, steals 8, blocks 5, fouls 19, overtime 6%, home wins 54-55%. Five automatic seasons (NBA alone and NBA + Spain): speed, save size (limit 32 MiB), champions spread, tax payers, NBA-Europe flows.
